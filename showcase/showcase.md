# Fathom

Carry a little light into a very deep dark. You are a tiny glowing forager, threading flooded stone corridors and grazing the plankton suspended in them. Every mouthful makes your world brighter—and makes you easier to find.

Your light uncovers the trench nearby. Send a sonar pulse around a bend to remember the route ahead, but keep swimming: the world beyond your growing circle slips back into darkness. A remembered corridor can save your dive, even when you can no longer see it.

Three hunters listen to different signals. Lanternjaw follows your glow beneath an innocent amber bell. Gloamfin hears the water and answers your sonar with its own. Flarefish lights the abyss in a sudden warm bloom. Drop ink to break a sightline, turn a corner to lose a pursuer, and learn which glimmers deserve your trust.

Eat every mote to clear the maze and descend. The deeper you go, the more hunters share your corridors and the shorter your sonar reaches. Keep your light alive long enough to find the next way down.
